Chronological History of India's Democratic Voting System

1. Ancient Roots (The Gana-Sanghas)

Long before modern ballot papers, democracy existed in ancient India around the 6th century BCE. Independent republics known as Gana-Sanghas (such as the Vajji confederation and Licchavis) made administrative decisions collectively. Voting was conducted using colored wooden pieces called Salakas.

2. 1950: Universal Adult Suffrage

When India adopted its Constitution on January 26, 1950, it took a historic step. India implemented Universal Adult Suffrage (Article 326). Every adult citizen, regardless of gender, religion, caste or wealth, received equal voting rights.

3. The 61st Amendment Act, 1988

Originally, the legal minimum voting age was 21 years. Parliament passed the 61st Constitutional Amendment Act in 1988, lowering the voting age to 18 years.
